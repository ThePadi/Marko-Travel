Calibangbangan Island · Palawan 🇵🇭

248 students. No running water.

I've visited this island many times. In November 2025, Typhoon Tino severely damaged the local school. These kids are not strangers to me. They are kids whose names I know.

Thanks to more than 40 donors, the first fundraiser raised over $4,200 and one classroom is being rebuilt with local volunteers. The next goal is clean water. Students and teachers carry water in buckets, and the nearest source of cleaner drinking water is about two kilometers away.

Almost everything has to arrive by boat from mainland Palawan, about an hour away. I'll keep sharing updates, videos, photos, receipts and progress directly from the island.
